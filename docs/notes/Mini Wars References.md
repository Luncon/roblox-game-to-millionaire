## Community wiki — added October 29, 2026

[Mini War Roblox Wiki — Home](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Home)

Added at the user's request as another gameplay reference alongside the building
guides, research guide and screenshots above. Use it to look up Mini War's
building roles, economy, shops, units and progression when adapting those ideas.
Direct page fetching returned HTTP 402/robots restrictions, but search-indexed
page content was available. The indexed home organizes factories, housing,
military, special buildings, technology, territories, quests and other systems.
These snapshots were crawled several months before this note; current numeric
values still need in-game confirmation before changing our balance.
Historical architecture and troop designs continue to use the separate dated
civilization source cards; this wiki supplies game reference material.

Useful follow-up pages:

- [Technology Tree](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Technology_Tree):
  linked research paths, costs, timers and exact effects; useful alongside the
  saved upgrade-tree screenshots and existing guide.
- [Houses](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Houses): population capacity,
  footprints and housing tiers; compare space efficiency rather than copying
  modern apartment/villa visuals into every civilization.
- [Special buildings](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Special): storage,
  automatic collection and production support; adapt functions to period stores,
  porters and work organization.
- [Game Systems](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Systems): production,
  private stocks, markets, civilians, territory and research overview. Its indexed
  text distinguishes a three-minute shared market from five-minute private stock;
  our three-minute personal merchant is a separate chosen prototype rule.
- [Game Mechanics](https://mini-war-roblox.fandom.com/wiki/Game_Mechanics): later
  territory logistics and defensive systems for future conquest planning.

The wiki informs interaction and progression references. It does not change the
agreed first-slice budgets or add its modern weapons, offline income, monetization
or territory systems to the current sandbox automatically.
