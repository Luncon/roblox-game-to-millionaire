# Proposal

## Why

Only some civilizations have imported hats/weapons, and players and generated
ranged troops still use placeholder body outfits. The user wants fitting free
Roblox marketplace looks for all thirteen civilizations and their weapon roles.

## What Changes

- Search the Creator Store with its free-only filter, verify importability,
  inspect and export lightweight inert outfit/weapon assets with provenance.
- Dress players and generated troops for every civilization. Carry existing
  user-provided custom melee outfit identity to their players/ranged troops,
  retaining the five custom melee skins and the confirmed Egyptian headwear split.
- Complete weapon visual coverage for all melee/ranged classes, keeping current
  server damage, range, progression, physics ownership and saves unchanged.
- Restore the player's original wardrobe when clearing civilization equipment;
  prevent late avatar appearance loading from overwriting equipped looks.
- Give generated melee/ranged troops classic rounded Roblox heads and faces;
  retain the existing custom melee head meshes and headwear.
- Assign each civilization an artistic NPC skin-tone palette and use a warrior
  face on both troop classes, including custom clones, through gear upgrades.
- Approximate historical styling is explicitly accepted. Paid acquisitions,
  new combat mechanics and replacing the five supplied melee skins are outside scope.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `civilization-outfits`: Complete safe civilization wardrobes for players and
  troops, with restored avatar appearance and free-asset attribution.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The existing equipment-visual change remains a completed baseline.

## Impact

Equipment rendering, normalized outfit/weapon assets and Rojo mappings,
appearance-loading integration, equipment regressions and Studio visual checks.
No dependency, economy or save schema changes.
