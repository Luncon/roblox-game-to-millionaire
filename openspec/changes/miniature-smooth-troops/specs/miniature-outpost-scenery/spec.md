# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Makes capturable settlements read as inhabited ancient frontier compounds while
preserving their existing capture objectives and traversable combat space.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Structured frontier settlements

Every initial capturable outpost SHALL include partial defensive walls or
palisades, an identifiable entrance, buildings and camp decoration at miniature
scale. The central ownership flag and capture display SHALL remain readable.
Scenery SHALL be anchored and bounded in quantity.

#### Scenario: Arriving at the first outpost

- **WHEN** a player approaches any first capturable outpost
- **THEN** visible walls, buildings and decoration identify a small settlement
- **AND** the central flag, capture bar and command interaction remain accessible
- **AND** the scenery fits inside the existing outpost site

### Requirement: Preserve clear capture approaches

Outpost scenery SHALL retain clear approach corridors, central fighting room and
the existing capture radius. Decorative geometry SHALL NOT block numeric troop
movement or change combat sight checks. New scenery SHALL NOT introduce purchases,
production, defensive damage or ownership authority.

#### Scenario: An army enters the decorated compound

- **WHEN** player troops approach the settlement and engage its defenders
- **THEN** they can reach the capture area without crossing closed decorative walls
- **AND** scenery does not intercept authoritative combat rays
- **AND** the20-second capture, defender pauses and E targeting behave as before
