# Civilization building kits

## Purpose

Give miniature settlements distinct, function-specific buildings and troop
presentations grounded in each of the thirteen civilizations' historical periods.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Retain the established historical roster

The redesign SHALL retain all thirteen existing civilizations and their reference
periods. Building and troop art SHALL identify its civilization and period rather
than treating every faction as belonging to a single ancient era.

#### Scenario: Select an existing civilization

- **WHEN** the player views a civilization in the miniature sandbox
- **THEN** its identity and period match the established roster
- **AND** selecting it does not convert or overwrite its production-game profile

### Requirement: Distinct architecture for each building function

Each civilization SHALL have a distinct design for housing, food production,
timber supply, craft-material production, artisan production, storage, trade,
melee training, ranged training, civic and medic support. Shared helpers SHALL NOT
reduce different civilizations to color variants of identical geometry.

#### Scenario: Compare building kits

- **WHEN** houses, workshops, stores and civic buildings are reviewed across the thirteen kits
- **THEN** silhouettes, roof construction, materials and structural proportions express the selected civilization
- **AND** function-specific props and geometry distinguish the roles within a kit
- **AND** Seneca bark longhouses and Viking timber halls use different structural designs

#### Scenario: Upgrade an ordinary building

- **WHEN** a workshop or house gains an upgrade
- **THEN** it retains its architectural family and practical building function
- **AND** it does not become a generic temple, palace, minaret or pagoda merely to signal higher level

### Requirement: Historical evidence accompanies shipped art

Every shipped civilization-role model SHALL record its period, region, source
links, observed features and interpretive choices. Later additions, modern
reconstructions and uncertain forms SHALL be labeled. A monumental reference
alone SHALL NOT establish historical accuracy for every domestic building.

#### Scenario: Review a building before release

- **WHEN** a new model is presented for acceptance
- **THEN** its evidence distinguishes source-supported features from original game design
- **AND** unresolved period or function conflicts prevent declaring the model historically verified
- **AND** source artwork and geometry provenance are recorded

### Requirement: Shared economic roles with civilization-specific presentation

Civilization variants SHALL preserve the initial shared price, capacity and
production budgets for their economic roles while allowing historically suitable
names, raw materials and recipes. Presentation alone SHALL NOT grant bonuses.

#### Scenario: Use a non-Spartan artisan economy

- **WHEN** a faction uses hides, fiber or another supported craft material in place of Spartan clay
- **THEN** its documented recipe preserves the same role budget
- **AND** labels, materials and work props match its own design kit
- **AND** no faction is required to use an inappropriate forge or mill to access the basic loop

### Requirement: Period-appropriate troop presentations

Initial military presentations SHALL distinguish frontline, missile and optional
support roles using faction-specific equipment. Commanders SHALL remain normal
size and unarmed. Mobility and siege candidates SHALL NOT be represented as
implemented combat unlocks in this economy sandbox.

#### Scenario: Inspect a faction's military capacity

- **WHEN** the player places the matching melee, ranged or medic building
- **THEN** a miniature faction-appropriate actor appears without granting the player a weapon
- **AND** no unimplemented damage, healing or conquest behavior is claimed by the UI
- **AND** placing the building does not grant an undocumented economic or combat bonus

### Requirement: Preserve scale and source models

New miniature art SHALL retain the quarter-scale preview direction, fit its
declared footprint and remain nonblocking to commanders. Reused rigs, equipment
and outfits SHALL be sanitized clones; source templates SHALL remain immutable.

#### Scenario: Verify a populated settlement in Studio

- **WHEN** the player walks around and through its miniature building area
- **THEN** the commander remains normal size with usable interaction targets and clear navigation lanes
- **AND** miniature joints, clothes and equipment remain aligned during movement
- **AND** source-template fingerprints match their pre-test values

### Requirement: Distinct ranged training yards

All thirteen kits SHALL include a quarter-scale ranged-yard design fitting the
10×8footprint and each culture's existing period/material vocabulary. Its
structure and training props SHALL distinguish it from the melee building, with
source-card links and inferred practice-yard details clearly labeled.

#### Scenario: Review the tenth building role

- **WHEN** the ranged building is created for each civilization
- **THEN** the model fits cardinal rotations and remains inert/nonblocking
- **AND** it differs geometrically from melee training and has a matching native preview
- **AND** troop equipment and source templates remain unchanged
